# Southeast Asian History & Civilizations — Interactive Mindmap

An interactive, collapsible mindmap covering Southeast Asian history and civilizations,
built as a single self-contained `index.html` (no build step, no dependencies).

## View it

- **Live (GitHub Pages):** https://ohshtnvm.github.io/southeast-asian-history-mindmap/
- **Locally:** open `index.html` in any browser.

## Interactions

- **Click** a node to expand / collapse its branch (`+` / `−`).
- Nodes with an info tooltip reveal a short note on click.
- **Drag** the background to pan; **scroll** to zoom (or use the `+ / − / ⤢` controls).
- **Expand all / Collapse all / Reset** buttons in the toolbar.

## The 7 branches

1. **Geography and Livelihood** — mainland vs. maritime SEA, rivers, forests, farming, fishing, minerals
2. **Human Geography and Migration** — ethnolinguistic groups, religion, social structure, migration theories
3. **Early Civilizations and Prehistory** — civilization, the Neolithic Revolution, Hoabinhian & Phung Nguyen cultures
4. **Mainland Ancient Kingdoms** — Funan, Chenla, Angkor, Pagan, Sukhothai, Ayutthaya, Dai Viet, Toungoo
5. **Maritime and Insular Civilizations** — Sri Vijaya, Majapahit, Malacca, Sailendra, and PH trade links
6. **Interrelatedness and Influences** — Indianization, Sinification, and regional exchange
7. **European Colonialism** — Age of Exploration and Portuguese, Spanish, Dutch & British colonialism

Content is adapted from a study outline on Southeast Asian history and civilizations.
